# 💫 About Me

I am a seasoned Software and Enterprise ERP Developer with over 8 years of experience building scalable systems across PHP, Java, Python, and Oracle PeopleSoft. My background covers enterprise-level architecture, relational databases like MySQL, Oracle-certified implementations, and analytics reporting with OAC (Oracle Analytics Cloud) and OBIEE, alongside modern full-stack web platforms and open-source tooling.

  * **Fogo** — Productivity extension designed to streamline browsing workflows.
  * **Affiscope** — Affiliate inspection and data analysis utility.

* **WordPress Ecosystem:**
  * **AffiStyle** — Specialized WordPress plugin for affiliate styling and site enhancement (*currently rolling out active feature and performance updates*).

* **Open-Source Packages (npm & GitHub):**
  * [**universal-form-validator**](https://github.com/GamersStop/universal-form-validator) — Lightweight, zero-dependency engine for structured form validation.
  * [**ai-prompt-firewall**](https://github.com/GamersStop/ai-prompt-firewall) — High-performance security middleware designed to intercept and sanitize prompts before reaching Large Language Models (LLMs).

* **Civic & Social Impact Platforms:**
  * [**Paw ID Care**](https://github.com/GamersStop/paw-id-care) — Multimodal civic-tech rescue and registration platform developed for the DEV.to Hackathon challenge.
  * [**Farmers Rural Economic Ecosystem Platform**](https://github.com/GamersStop/farmers-rural-economic-ecosystem) — Digital infrastructure engineered to empower rural producers and farmers with open market access.
